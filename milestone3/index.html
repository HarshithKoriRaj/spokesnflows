Spokes & Flows: Milestone 3

A working first-draft D3 narrative for the Citi Bike network-planning questions.

This first draft uses January, July, and October 2025 trip data to show where Citi Bike systems strain, when riders are active, how trips move between neighborhoods, and how weather relates to ridership. The design keeps the four core questions in a readable narrative and makes station net flow the central comparison.

Q1. When do people ride, and how do members and casual riders differ?

The chart below compares weekday-hour activity by rider type using trips per day. We normalize by the number of times each weekday occurs so the comparison reflects exposure rather than raw volume alone.

Q2. Where and when do stations gain or lose bikes through trips?

The view shows a zero-centered net-flow comparison per station and dock, with the selected hour and day type adjusting the ranking. Negative values mean net losses; positive values mean gains.

Q3. Where do the trips behind that imbalance come from?

The flow ranking shows the busiest neighborhood-to-neighborhood trip pairs for each period, helping explain which origins and destinations drive the station imbalances above.

Q4. How much do weather and season change ridership?

This scatter plot compares daily high temperature against the number of trips as a percent of that month’s average day. Rainy days are highlighted separately from dry days, and the series are shown by rider type.

Q5 (optional). Do e-bike trips differ from classic-bike trips?

The histogram shows straight-line trip distance by bike type; the final bin, 8 km or more, is kept separate to prevent distortion from the open-ended tail.

AI use statement: the team used Claude to help draft data-cleaning logic and chart mockups, while the final implementation and interpretation in this repository were reviewed and revised by the team.
